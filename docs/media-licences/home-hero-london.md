## Brief

A slow, locked-off or very slowly drifting shot of the City / Canary Wharf
skyline or a single tower elevation. 10–20 seconds, silent, looping without a
visible cut. Graded dark enough that the headline sits over it at 4.5:1 — the
hero applies a scrim, but the scrim is not a rescue for a bright plate.

## Notes

Stock drone footage of London is widely available and widely mislicensed. Two
specific traps:

- Free-tier downloads from the large stock libraries frequently exclude
  financial services, or exclude use in a context implying endorsement.
- Footage containing identifiable branded buildings can carry separate
  restrictions from the footage licence itself.

If the firm commissions the shot instead, the commissioning contract must assign
or licence copyright to Guide explicitly — paying for a shoot does not transfer
copyright by default under UK law.
